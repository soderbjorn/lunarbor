# 3D mode

Feature spec moved out of `CLAUDE.md` to keep it small. Keep it current when you change the feature.

## 3D mode

Five shapes (`web/.../main/space/`, LBR-11): **Pages**, its whole-vault variant **Grove**, and three maps — **Crown**, **Cone**, **Galaxy** (below). **Off by default** (on by default in the browser demo, while the key is absent): App settings → Experimental → "Enable 3D mode" (`SpaceMode.setEnabled`, persister key `lunarborSpaceEnabled`, read as `isSpaceModeEnabled`) turns it on, like Lunamux's "Enable 3D app switcher"; while off the topbar planet (a ringed planet icon) is hidden (CSS, `body[data-lunarbor-space-enabled]`), ⌃⌘3 / ⌃⌘1 / ⌥⌘F do nothing and the Keyboard Shortcuts sidebar leaves out their rows; turning it off leaves 3D mode. Every node's page hangs at a fixed place in space, and each window looks into it through its own camera. Toggled by the topbar planet, ⌃⌘3 (Ctrl-Alt-3 off the Mac), the palette's "Enter 3D mode" / "Leave 3D mode" (only while enabled) and Esc; remembered under the persister key `lunarborSpace` (`{ split, shape, spread, bundle }`) — app state, never the vault; the app always starts in 2D, never in 3D mode. The shape is picked in the strip's switcher (Pages / Grove / Crown / Cone / Galaxy), with ⌃⌘2 (next shape) or L on a map (`SpaceMode.setShape`, `SpaceShape`). The strip's **Help** button (and `?` on a map) opens a scrollable dialog explaining the mode and every view with all its controls (`space/SpaceHelp.kt`, `showSpaceHelp`; static text — keep it in step with what the views do).

- **Full window:** the space is a layer over the whole window (`.lunarbor-space`, z-index 900): sidebar, top bar and panes are under it; menus, popups, modals and the palette still open above. A slim draggable strip on top holds the shape switcher, Help, the single / split switch and Leave 3D (⌘P still opens the palette); in Pages a dock along the bottom lists the tabs (click to switch) and the active tab's windows (`1 · Recipes`, click to focus); the maps hide it.
- **Single or split** (⌃⌘1, the strip button): the focused window alone, filling the space, or every window of the tab at exactly its 2D place and size — read from the toolkit's drawn panes (`AppShell.spaceHost.spacePanes`), stacked by z-order; focusing a view raises its window as a click does in 2D. Each view has its own camera and flies on its own.
- **The live page is the pane's real `MainScreen`** (`MainScreen.mountInSpace` / `leaveSpace`), reparented into a CSS3D object at 1:1 — there is no second editor: typing, Tab, Enter, folding, search, palette commands, drawings and images all run the 2D code. In space the navigation and fold animations are off (the flight replaces them) and Escape leaves 3D instead of clearing the zoom; flying out is ⌃⌘↑, Back or the page header's breadcrumb. Leaving 3D puts every editor back with its caret and scroll.
- **Pages** fit their content (a transparent page-sized slot holds a card at most that tall; a drawing or web page fills it). Child pages hang behind the live page in a left and a right column, grandchildren in one column on their parent's outer side, faint; threads (SVG) run from each child bullet's dot to its page. Previews are read-only outlines (`PageSpaceModel`: from the open outline's rows, so an indent sprouts a page at once; else from `DocumentRegistry.requestLinkPreview` listings); clicking one, or a dot in it, zooms the window there. Round them hangs the rest of the vault, where free flight puts it (`PageSpaceLayout.wholeLayout` anchored at the window's page — siblings, other branches), once every listing has landed; a page that would show over the window's own (level with it or nearer, overlapping it — other branches' columns can cross it) is left out (`staysBehind`), so the focused page is always in front. Only the 24 nearest (children first) show their bullets, the next 48 as title cards and the rest as WebGL rectangles (`NAV_FULL` / `NAV_SLABS`), so a node with hundreds of grandchildren flies smoothly; threads to a page that shows behind the live page are left out. What the privacy mode hides (LBR-10) is never a page or a preview row (`spacePageOf` applies `hiddenRowsIn` and filters listings as link previews do); the live page shows backlinks (LBR-7) like the pane does.
- **Layout and model are pure commonMain** (`main/PageSpaceLayout.kt`, tested in `PageSpaceLayoutTest`): `PageSpaceLayout` places pages relative to the page a window is on (deterministic, so Back returns exactly where it was); `PaneBackingViewModel.spacePageOf` gives the page around a pane, keyed by node folder (`PageSpaceKeys`) so a node is one page whether zoomed into or opened. The camera follows pane state: a change of `(activeFileRel, zoomedLineId)` to another page flies (0.75–1.5 s; a cut under `prefers-reduced-motion`); editing never moves it.
- **Rendering rules** (`PageSpaceView`): the three.js camera never moves (the world does — Chrome stops hit-testing a CSS3D layer whose camera has moved); pages get z-indexes by distance and pages behind the camera ignore the pointer; stray `scrollTop` is reset every frame; one WebGL canvas draws every view's starfield via viewport + scissor; frames run only while something moves. Colours and fonts are the theme's `--t-*` / `--dt-font-prop` variables; the WebGL specks re-read them on theme change (glow on dark, ink on light).
- **Grove** (`space/GroveView.kt`, layout in commonMain `main/GroveLayout.kt`, tested in `GroveLayoutTest`): Pages laid out over the whole vault. Every node with children is a slab page (CSS-3D edges and back, `GroveLayout.THICKNESS`; a preview grows with its bullets up to `PREVIEW_HEIGHT_FACTOR` slots) at a fixed place and angle (`SpacePose`, `SpaceQuat`): Home at the origin facing the camera; radial from it, every subtree owns a cell of directions (an ordered treemap on the sphere between `HOLE` and `CAP` off Home's axis, shares by the cube root of a branch's pages), each page at its cell's roomiest spot, a step further out than its parent and far enough to fit its cell, facing its parent — so no two pages overlap and a big branch pushes only its own deeper pages out; deterministic, so Back returns exactly. The tree is `GroveLayout.treeOf`: `SpaceMode.pagesGraph` (the maps' `VaultGraphBuilder` graph under the current privacy filter) with the window's live page (`spacePageOf`) spliced in, so an indent sprouts a page at once; until every listing has landed a view lays out its page and children alone, then cuts to the whole vault. The camera turns: the world group gets the inverse of the camera's pose; navigation flies along a curve, rotation slerped, to face the page square on at 1:1 (`GroveLayout.cameraFor`). Near the window's page (children, grandchildren, ancestors, siblings, at most `MAX_FULL`) pages are read-only previews; the `MAX_SLABS` nearest others are title-only slabs; no threads. Its starfield is a shell all round (`Backdrop`'s round field), following a fraction of the camera's travel.
- **Free flight in Pages and Grove** (`space/PageFlight.kt`): ⌥⌘F (Ctrl-Alt-F off the Mac; ⌃⌘4 too; rebindable, `AppShell.SPACE_FLY_ACTION`, `SpaceMode.toggleFlight` — a plain F would type into the editor, ⌃⌘F is macOS's full screen) or the strip's **Fly** button turns the focused view's camera into the maps' ship (`FreeFlight`, same keys; `MapLegend` with only the free-flight panel). Nothing edits the document while flying: a capture-phase `window` listener takes every key, single-modifier chords too (paste, undo, palette commands; the editor is blurred), passing only Escape and two-modifier chords (navigation, 3D mode's own), and a click on the live page lands without placing a caret. Title-only cards (`.is-slab`) drop the glow shadows, which made flying slow. The page ahead (`PageFlight.aim`) has its border lit (`.is-aimed`, no ring); Enter or a click opens it in the window — the camera holds where the ship stopped until the navigation arrives (`holdUntil`, else it lands), so that flight starts from the ship. Landing (F, C, ⌥⌘F) flies the camera back to face the window's page, the one it is zoomed to; in Pages it also turns upright again (Pages' camera turns only in and after free flight, and shows the round starfield meanwhile). In Pages, flying paints the whole vault by Pages' rules (`PageSpaceLayout.wholeLayout`, anchored at the window's page, over `GroveLayout.treeOf`): the `FLIGHT_FULL` pages nearest the ship as previews, the next `FLIGHT_SLABS` as title cards, every other page as a WebGL card without text — a title card's shape and colours: the editor's background, an outline and top edge in its area's hue — drawn with the starfield, far to near (`FarPages`, `SpaceWindowView.backdropExtra`), re-ranked every `FLIGHT_SYNC_S`; in Grove the slabs follow the ship. Every card in both carries the same header (`space/SpacePageHead.kt`, `fillSpaceHead`: the pane header's Back / Forward / Up icons, `AppShell.ICON_BACK` …, and the page's breadcrumb — working on the live page; dimmed arrows and a clickable path from the folder, `folderCrumbs`, on previews) and the page's big title (`.lunarbor-space-preview-headline`, styled like `.lunarbor-title`), so nothing pops in or out in a flight.
- **Maps** (`space/MapView.kt`; Crown, Cone, Galaxy): the vault's node tree as bodies — the root and every folder-backed item, sized by what is under them, leaf bullets as dust around them — with branches to their parents, vault links between bodies — bundled along the tree by default (hierarchical edge bundling, `main/LinkBundling.kt`, tested in `LinkBundlingTest`: a B-spline through the bodies from one end up to the lowest common ancestor and down, pulled straight by `1 − DEFAULT_BETA`, so links between the same areas run as one strand; B switches to free arcs, remembered as `bundle` in `lunarborSpace`) — and the tab's windows as cards (the page's title) joined by a line to the body each shows. The graph (`main/VaultGraph.kt`, `VaultGraphBuilder`) comes from the registry's node listings (`requestLinkPreview`, asked folder by folder; only a progress card shows until every listing has landed, then the map appears whole; capped at `MAX_NODES`, 5000) and the link index (`DocumentRegistry.linkIndexSnapshot`, read only once every listing has landed — its vault scan would otherwise queue the listings behind it on a slow, cloud-synced disk); positions from `main/GraphLayout.kt` (pure, deterministic from paths and sibling order, tested in `VaultGraphTest`): Crown grows up and out from the root (depth is height, each area a slice of the circle), Cone hangs each node's children in a ring below it, Galaxy is a force layout seeded from the crown where links pull too. Folds are the map's own (Space, or a click on a body or its label; O (or 0) folds every node but the root, X unfolds all, `.` unfolds the shallowest folded level on screen, `,` folds the deepest open one; a folded body wears a ring; the map starts with every node unfolded). Drag orbits, right- or Shift-drag pans, scroll zooms; click a body to select it and fold / unfold it (unfolding flies the camera to frame the body and every body now showing under it, just filling the view, `frameSubtree`; folding holds it still; the arrow keys fly), ⏎ opens it in the focused window (`navigateToLink`), a double-click (its two clicks fold and unfold, so the fold is unchanged), P or the selection bar's "Edit page" opens it and switches to Pages once the window has arrived, to edit it, E goes back to Pages, ← → ↑ ↓ walk siblings / parent / child, C resets the camera (no selection, the starting angle, the whole map); zooming in past the closest orbit moves forward; − / + narrow or widen the space between bodies (`spread`, 1–8×, default 2.5×, sizes unchanged; remembered in `lunarborSpace`). A keyboard legend sits in the bottom left (`space/MapLegend.kt`, Lunamux-style; K hides it). **Free flight** (`space/FreeFlight.kt`, Lunamux's ship model and keys): F takes off from the orbit camera's pose — W / S throttle, A / D strafe, R / V rise / descend, ↑ ↓ pitch, ← → yaw, Q / E roll, C lands and resets the camera, Enter edits the body in the dashed ring ahead, Esc leaves 3D — and F lands, the orbit carrying on from the ship's place. Labels are DOM (at most 40, greedy, never overlapping; tags left out, as in breadcrumbs). WebGL instanced spheres + line buffers through `ThreeLib.raw` (dynamic, kept inside `MapView`); the map's camera moves freely (no CSS3D layer there). **Privacy:** the graph is built with the current mode's filter — an item with a hiding tag, or a hidden folder, is left out with its subtree; a link whose file or target is hidden is dropped — and rebuilt when `privacyFlow` changes.
- **Colour** (`main/SpacePalette.kt`): 3D mode is deliberately more colourful than the theme. The areas are the children of the first node that branches (a single-child chain from the root — Home → Main — is skipped and stays neutral); each area gets a vivid hue (golden-angle steps in unfiltered outline order, so a privacy mode never shifts a colour) and each branch below an area a hue of its own from anywhere on the wheel, deeper nodes varying a little around it, branches take their child's hue, link arcs blend between their ends' hues, pages in Pages wear it as a top edge and glow, item dots and threads (`SpaceMode.areaColor`), and some of the background specks are tinted. The theme still decides background, text and chrome.
- **three.js** (`npm three 0.170.0`) loads lazily on first entry through a dynamic `import()` into its own chunk (`space/three/ThreeLib.kt`: minimal externals, kept free of Lunarbor types for a later move to Lunula).
