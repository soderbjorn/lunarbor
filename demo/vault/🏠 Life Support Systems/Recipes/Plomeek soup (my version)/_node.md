> **Serves 2 Vulcans or 1 hungry developer**
>
> * 2 carrots, 1 parsnip, 1 small orange squash
> * 1 onion, 2 cloves garlic
> * 1 l vegetable stock
> * a pinch of chili (unauthentic, but logical)
>
> Roast the vegetables at 200 °C for 30 minutes, blend with the stock, season. Serve in a bowl you will not throw at the wall.
